# Monitoring Console

Mode: Operate. Extend the established dashboard, not its visual identity.

## Direction Contract

THESIS: Show the evidence and its limits. Expand inspection depth without turning
the public console into an unrestricted metrics browser.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit Carbon g100, Plex, square tiles, muted gray surfaces, and the
existing blue/teal charts and semantic green/red states.

STORY: Scan current network state, choose a history window, trace a concern through
charts and searchable tables, and sign in for private diagnostics.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact heading and snapshot age, sticky navigation and time controls,
six summary readings, then topology beside BGP history. Operator access stays in
the same vertical reading path.

FORM: Local extension of the existing Carbon console; no concept roll or replacement
world. Mobile stacks panels and keeps wide tables inside keyboard-scrollable regions.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
